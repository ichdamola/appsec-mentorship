# Week 16: Threat Modeling Capstone

## 🎯 What you'll learn

Bring it together. Pick a real (or realistic) application architecture; produce a STRIDE-based threat model; identify and prioritize mitigations; integrate into the SDLC.

By the end of this week you'll be able to:
- STRIDE methodology
- Data flow diagrams as the threat-modeling artifact
- Attack trees for high-value flows
- Integrating threat modeling into product development (when to threat-model, who owns it)
