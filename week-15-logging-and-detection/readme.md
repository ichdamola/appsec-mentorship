# Week 15: Logging, Monitoring, and Detection

## 🎯 What you'll learn

OWASP A09: you can't respond to what you can't see. Build the logs and detections that catch the first 14 weeks worth of attacks.

By the end of this week you'll be able to:
- What to log (and what NOT to log — passwords, tokens)
- Detection rules for the attacks in weeks 1-14
- Alerting fatigue and tuning
- Incident response basics — IR is a separate curriculum but understand the handoff
